HearAssist Privacy Policy / 개인정보처리방침

Policy date / 정책 기준일: 2026-10-23

English · 한국어

HearAssist is a manual ambient sound-control tool for adults aged 18 or older with normal hearing. It processes nearby sound from the phone microphone on the device and sends it to headphones you select. It is not intended to compensate for hearing loss, replace hearing aids, test hearing, diagnose or treat a condition.

HearAssist는 일반 청력의 성인(18세 이상)을 위한 주변 소리 수동 조절 도구입니다. 휴대전화 마이크로 들어온 소리를 기기 안에서 처리해 직접 선택한 이어폰으로 보냅니다. 청력 손실 보정·보청기 대체·청력검사·진단·치료를 목적으로 제공하지 않습니다.

Only a listening session you start processes sound from the phone microphone. Audio stays in on-device memory buffers and goes to your selected headphones. It is not saved as recording files or sent to servers, advertising or analytics services. There are no accounts, payments, ads or tracking SDKs.

Gain, left/right trim and tone settings are stored on this device. Reset settings restores defaults. Clear storage in Android app information or uninstall the app to remove internal data. Android app data backup is disabled. The app also stores whether microphone permission was denied, to choose the next permission guidance. Resetting sound controls does not clear this permission history.

If you explicitly select an advanced 8-second trial, the app stores one latest numeric receipt containing device, frame, error and setting data. A new trial replaces it. It contains no raw audio, but some numbers may reveal voice activity. Ordinary listening does not save this receipt. There is no automatic upload or sharing.

Stop listening from the screen or notification. Revoke microphone access in Android app permissions. Permission approval, reconnection or reopening the app does not start listening. Confirm local consent before relaying other people’s voices. Information you send to the public contact email may be processed by the email provider.

Android and the connected headphones manage Bluetooth output. This is not app cloud transmission. The app does not request Bluetooth pairing or scanning, location, contacts, phone or storage permissions. Android and headphone vendors apply their own data policies.

This public support website is separate from the offline Android app. The hosting provider may process IP addresses, request time and browser details to serve HTTPS pages and protect the site. No microphone audio or Android trial receipt is sent to this site. No cookies, analytics scripts, advertising, account forms or audio upload features are added by this website. Hosting is provided by GitHub Pages under its own privacy policy: GitHub privacy statement.

Support email is optional. Correspondence is used to answer the request and is deleted when it is no longer needed, except records that must be retained under applicable law. Do not send recordings or sensitive health information. For access or deletion inquiries, contact db0192a@gmail.com. The publisher does not receive app audio or settings to retrieve or delete remotely.
