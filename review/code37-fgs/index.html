HearAssist foreground microphone demonstration

Release candidate 37 (0.7.9-no-rnnoise), package org.hearassist.prototype. Recorded on a physical Galaxy Note10 (Android 12), using a selected Buds3 A2DP output.

Muted engineering demonstration. The phone microphone, input FIFO and on-device product processing run normally, but the final output is DIGITAL_ZERO. This video has no audio track and contains no recorded microphone audio. It does not demonstrate listening quality, acoustic level, latency or xrun continuity.

Open the MP4 directly

  1. The user explicitly taps the visible consent/Start button. Granting microphone permission alone does not start the session.
  2. The actual microphone foreground service remains active after leaving the app. The Android microphone indicator remains visible.
  3. The app is reopened and the visible Stop button is tapped. Microphone use and the foreground service close; no automatic restart follows.

The optional eight-second local test limit was enabled as a safety fallback. In this clip, manual Stop was observed before that limit. Route and focus protections remain part of the app; this clip alone is not proof of every safety transition or Google Play approval.
